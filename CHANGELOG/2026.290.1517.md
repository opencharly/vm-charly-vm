## Round 1 — one finding, real, and it was inside the file this leg edits

**`AGENTS.md` contradicted itself three lines apart.** The intro sentence above `Canonical files:` still read *"the load-bearing content is the sibling `skill:` nodes in `charly.yml`"* — while this PR's own edit to the very next bullet asserted those nodes are **routing metadata only** and that the bodies live under `.agents/skills/`. Both statements were in the same file, in the same commit, and the intro one was false. Fixed: the intro now names the **bodies** as the load-bearing content and the nodes as the metadata, and the sibling sentence about projection says "the skill **bodies** are PROJECTED" rather than "the `skill:` entities are".

**The missed control, and it is tonight's class again, one turn further:** my R5 sweep was keyed to **one phrasing** of the retired wording — `git grep -n 'embedded \`skill:\`'` → `rc=1` — and I reported that as R5 discharged. **R5 is about outdated CLAIMS, not about a string.** A different sentence asserting the same thing ("the load-bearing content is the nodes") was never in the sweep's scope, so a clean grep certified a file that contradicted itself. The sweep is re-keyed to the **claim**, and it is pasted rather than described:

```
$ grep -rniE 'load-bearing|the .skill:. (entities|nodes) (is|are)|content is the' AGENTS.md README.md
AGENTS.md:8:the load-bearing content is the sibling skills' BODIES, authored under
rc=0
$ grep -rniE 'nodes in .charly.yml. (carry|carries|hold|holds)' AGENTS.md README.md
AGENTS.md:9:`.agents/skills/<name>/SKILL.md`; their nodes in `charly.yml` carry the routing
rc=0
```

Note what the re-keyed sweep returns *after* the fix: the corrected sentences themselves. **That is the calibration property** — a predicate that matches the claim when it is present is one whose empty result would mean something. Tonight has produced this lesson five times in five guises (a two-space grep, a family prefix, a wording sweep, a too-narrow quantifier, and now a claim swept as a phrase); the rule that covers all five is the one written into this leg: **sweep for the claim, and prove the predicate catches the claim when it is there.**
